# Sections

[[menu.layout > menu.layout.sections]] divides the drawing into parts with
their own page numbering, as books have: the front matter in roman
numerals, the text in arabic numerals starting again at 1.

Each section starts on a page and goes on until the next one. For each
one choose its [[section.numbering]], the number of its first page
([[section.start_at]]) and a [[section.name]]. [[section.book]] sets the
usual book: lower roman from page 1 and arabic from the page in view.

Page number fields (Page > Insert Page Number on a master layer) follow
the sections. [[menu.layout > menu.layout.running_head]] puts a field at
the top of the pages that shows the title of each page's section.
Without sections, page numbers work as before.
